## Purpose

This folder records the authorship, commercial delivery terms, intellectual-property position, confidentiality obligations, and operating conditions applicable to the MEC digital platform developed under Aöthothe Technologies.

The package is designed to establish a clear written record that:

1. Chrispine Mndala is identified as the principal technology author, system architect, and lead developer of the MEC platform, subject to any documented third-party or collaborator contributions.
2. Aöthothe Technologies is the technology provider responsible for the development, configuration, documentation, deployment support, and technical handover services described in signed project documents.
3. Mtendere Education Consult retains ownership of its business name, brand assets, organizational content, student and client information, and all materials it supplies.
4. Software ownership, licensing, source-code access, payment, support, confidentiality, data protection, and handover obligations must be governed by a signed written agreement rather than assumptions.
5. Open-source software and third-party services remain subject to their own licences and provider terms.

## Document Hierarchy

Where documents conflict, the following order should apply unless the parties expressly agree otherwise in writing:

1. A signed amendment or change order;
2. A signed software development and handover agreement;
3. A signed statement of work, quotation, invoice acceptance, or implementation schedule;
4. The intellectual-property and licence agreement;
5. The confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement;
6. The platform terms and conditions;
7. General project documentation.

## Execution Requirements

Before signature, the parties should complete all blank fields, confirm the correct legal names and registration details, identify authorized representatives, attach the final scope and payment schedule, and obtain independent legal review in Malawi.

Electronic signatures and signed counterparts may be used where legally valid and accepted by both parties.
