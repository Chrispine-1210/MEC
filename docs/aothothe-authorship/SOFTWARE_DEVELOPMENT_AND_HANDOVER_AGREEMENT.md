## 1. Purpose

The Client appoints Aöthothe to design, develop, configure, integrate, document, deploy, support, and hand over the Mtendere Education Consult digital platform in accordance with this Agreement and the approved project documents.

The platform may include a public website, student or client portal, administrative management system, database, backend services, integrations, authentication, communications, analytics, documentation, and deployment configuration.

---

## 2. Contract Documents and Priority

The Agreement consists of:

1. this signed Agreement;
2. any signed statement of work, quotation, invoice acceptance, implementation schedule, or change order;
3. the Intellectual Property and Licence Agreement;
4. the Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement;
5. the Platform Terms and Conditions;
6. approved specifications, acceptance criteria, and handover records.

If documents conflict, a later signed amendment or change order prevails, followed by the order listed above.

## 3. Scope of Services

Aöthothe shall provide only the services and deliverables identified in the signed scope or approved quotation. Unless expressly included, the following are outside scope:

- unlimited redesigns or feature revisions;
- third-party licence, hosting, domain, email, SMS, payment, AI, cloud, or transaction fees;
- data entry or migration beyond the agreed quantity and format;
- legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, immigration, or education-placement advice;
- continuous security monitoring or guaranteed incident response;
- hardware, connectivity, staff devices, or office-network services;
- new modules requested after scope approval;
- support after the included warranty or support period.

---

## 4. Project Governance

### 4.1 Authorized Representatives

Each Party shall nominate one authorized project representative. Instructions, approvals, rejections, and change requests issued by those representatives may be relied upon by the other Party.

### 4.2 Client Decisions

The Client shall provide approvals, content, credentials, access, feedback, and decisions within the periods stated in the project plan. Delay by the Client may extend delivery dates and increase cost.

### 4.3 Records

Project decisions may be evidenced through signed documents, approved email, project-management records, repository history, meeting minutes, or other agreed written communication.

---

## 5. Client Responsibilities

The Client shall:

- provide accurate business requirements and lawful content;
- appoint authorized reviewers and decision-makers;
- provide access to domains, hosting, email, databases, payment accounts, APIs, and other required services;
- maintain secure control over credentials and promptly rotate credentials after handover;
- verify all public content, fees, scholarship information, job information, partner claims, policies, and legal notices before publication;
- obtain all required consents for personal data, photographs, testimonials, and third-party materials;
- perform user acceptance testing and report defects with sufficient detail;
- pay all agreed amounts when due;
- use the platform lawfully and in accordance with the applicable licence.

The Client warrants that materials and instructions it supplies do not knowingly infringe third-party rights or violate applicable law.

## 7. Change Control

Any change affecting scope, cost, timeline, architecture, security, data processing, integrations, or acceptance criteria must be recorded in a written change order.

A change order should identify:

- requested change;
- business reason;
- technical impact;
- price adjustment;
- timeline adjustment;
- acceptance criteria;
- approving representatives.

Aöthothe is not required to begin out-of-scope work before written approval.

---

## 8. Delivery and Acceptance

### 8.1 Delivery

A deliverable is delivered when it is made available through a repository, staging environment, production environment, file transfer, documentation package, or other agreed method.

### 8.2 Acceptance Testing

The Client shall test each material deliverable within **ten business days** after delivery, unless another period is stated in the project plan.

The Client shall either:

1. confirm acceptance in writing; or
2. reject the deliverable in writing, identifying material non-conformities against the agreed scope and acceptance criteria.

### 8.3 Correction

Aöthothe shall use reasonable efforts to correct verified material defects that fall within scope. Requests for new functionality, changed preferences, content amendments, or third-party provider changes are not defects.

### 8.4 Deemed Acceptance

A deliverable may be treated as accepted where the Client:

- confirms acceptance;
- uses it in production for ordinary business operations;
- instructs Aöthothe to proceed to the next dependent milestone; or
- does not issue a compliant rejection within the acceptance period.

Deemed acceptance does not remove any express warranty obligation.

---

## 9. Handover

Subject to full payment and satisfaction of the agreed handover conditions, Aöthothe shall provide the applicable items included in scope, which may include:

- source-code repository access or an agreed source-code archive;
- production deployment and environment records;
- database and migration documentation;
- domain, hosting, email, and integration inventory;
- administrator account structure;
- operational manuals and training materials;
- known-issues register;
- backup and recovery guidance;
- third-party dependency and licence record;
- final acceptance or handover certificate.

Secrets, passwords, private keys, recovery codes, and tokens must be transferred using a secure method and should not be committed to a public repository or ordinary documentation.

## 11. Data Protection and Security

### 11.1 Roles

For personal data collected through the Client’s operations, the Client ordinarily determines the purposes and means of processing and remains responsible for lawful collection, notices, consents, retention decisions, and data-subject requests. Aöthothe processes such data only to the extent required to deliver or support the platform, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

### 11.2 Security Measures

Each Party shall implement reasonable safeguards appropriate to its role, including access control, credential management, backups, software updates, least-privilege administration, and incident reporting.

### 11.3 Security Limitations

No internet-connected system is completely secure. Aöthothe does not warrant that the platform will be immune from all attacks, outages, fraud, user error, third-party failures, or newly discovered vulnerabilities.

### 11.4 Incident Cooperation

Each Party shall promptly notify the other of a confirmed incident materially affecting the platform, credentials, confidential information, or personal data and shall reasonably cooperate in containment, investigation, remediation, and legally required notices.

### 16.3 Client Operations

## 18. Term and Termination

### 18.1 Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until completion, termination, or replacement by a later signed agreement.

### 18.2 Termination for Breach

A Party may terminate for material breach if the other Party fails to cure the breach within fourteen days after written notice, or within another reasonable period stated in the notice.

### 18.3 Immediate Termination

A Party may terminate immediately for fraud, unlawful use, serious confidentiality breach, insolvency, deliberate security abuse, or persistent non-payment after notice.

### 18.4 Consequences

On termination:

- the Client shall pay for completed work, approved commitments, and non-cancellable third-party costs;
- each Party shall return or securely dispose of Confidential Information where required;
- licences dependent on full payment do not become effective until payment is completed;
- clauses intended to survive shall remain effective, including payment, confidentiality, intellectual property, liability, dispute resolution, and records.

---

## 19. Force Majeure

Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control, including power failure, national connectivity failure, natural disaster, civil disturbance, government action, epidemic, major cloud outage, payment-network failure, or third-party infrastructure disruption.

The affected Party shall notify the other and use reasonable efforts to reduce the impact.

The Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation between authorized senior representatives.

If unresolved within fourteen days, the Parties shall consider mediation before commencing formal proceedings, unless urgent protective relief is required.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malawi. Subject to any agreed mediation or arbitration clause, the courts of Malawi shall have jurisdiction.
