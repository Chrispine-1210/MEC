# Intellectual Property and Software Licence Agreement

## Mtendere Education Consult Digital Platform

This Intellectual Property and Software Licence Agreement (the **“IP Agreement”**) is entered into on the ____ day of __________________ 20____ between:

- **Aöthothe Technologies**, represented by **Chrispine Mndala** (**“Aöthothe”**); and
- **Mtendere Education Consult** (**“MEC”** or the **“Client”**).

This IP Agreement forms part of the Software Development, Deployment and Handover Agreement for the MEC digital platform.

---

## 1. Objectives

The Parties intend to:

1. preserve a reliable record of authorship and contribution;
2. distinguish Client Materials, Aöthothe Background IP, Project Deliverables, and Third-Party Materials;
3. define the Client’s operational licence after payment;
4. prevent unauthorized resale, misrepresentation, or extraction of proprietary components;
5. ensure that the Client can lawfully operate and maintain the delivered platform.

---

## 2. Definitions

**“Aöthothe Background IP”** means software, tools, libraries, templates, methods, architectures, workflows, know-how, documentation structures, reusable components, scripts, designs, and materials owned, controlled, or developed by Aöthothe or Chrispine Mndala independently of the Client’s unique materials, including improvements of general application.

**“Client Materials”** means the Client’s name, logo, branding, content, records, photographs, documents, policies, business data, student or applicant data, partner information, and other materials supplied or controlled by the Client.

**“Project Deliverables”** means the custom configuration, implementation, code, documentation, database structures, workflows, and other items expressly identified for delivery under a signed project document.

**“Third-Party Materials”** means open-source software, commercial software, external APIs, cloud services, media, fonts, libraries, and other materials owned by third parties.

**“Source Code”** means the human-readable source files, configuration, schemas, scripts, and documentation required to maintain the delivered platform, excluding secrets and materials that Aöthothe has no right to transfer.

---

## 3. Authorship

The Parties acknowledge that **Chrispine Mndala, operating under Aöthothe Technologies, is the principal technology author, system architect, and lead developer of the MEC digital platform**, except for:

- Client Materials;
- Third-Party Materials;
- independently created contributions identified in writing;
- work expressly proven to have another author.

Authorship recognition is separate from the economic licence granted to the Client. Transfer of a copy, repository access, deployment access, or payment does not by itself erase or transfer authorship.

---

## 4. Ownership of Client Materials

The Client owns or controls all rights in Client Materials and grants Aöthothe a limited, non-exclusive licence to use them solely for development, testing, deployment, support, documentation, and approved portfolio use.

The Client warrants that it has the authority to supply and authorize use of Client Materials.

On termination or written request, Aöthothe shall cease unnecessary use of Client Materials, subject to legal retention, backup cycles, audit records, and continuing support obligations.

---

## 5. Ownership of Aöthothe Background IP

All Aöthothe Background IP remains the exclusive property of Aöthothe Technologies or Chrispine Mndala, as applicable.

Aöthothe may reuse general skills, methods, concepts, non-client-specific workflows, reusable code patterns, templates, and know-how in other projects, provided that it does not disclose Client Confidential Information, personal data, or uniquely identifying Client Materials.

---

## 6. Project Deliverables and Licence Grant

### 6.1 Licence Effective on Full Payment

Upon full payment of all undisputed amounts due for the applicable Project Deliverables, Aöthothe grants the Client a **perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence** to:

- host and operate the delivered MEC platform;
- use it for the Client’s internal and customer-facing business activities;
- copy it for backup, disaster recovery, staging, testing, and lawful maintenance;
- modify it internally or through an appointed professional maintainer;
- deploy it to infrastructure controlled by the Client or its authorized service provider.

### 6.2 Licence Restrictions

Unless separately authorized in writing, the Client shall not:

- sell, sublicense, white-label, or commercially distribute the platform as a standalone software product to unrelated third parties;
- represent itself or another person as the original author of Aöthothe-created work;
- publish proprietary Source Code in a public repository;
- extract Aöthothe Background IP for use in an unrelated competing software product;
- remove or falsify copyright, authorship, provenance, or licence notices from internal project records and Source Code;
- use the platform for unlawful, fraudulent, abusive, or rights-infringing purposes.

### 6.3 Business Transfer

The Client may transfer the operational licence with a genuine sale or transfer of substantially all of the MEC business that uses the platform, provided the successor agrees in writing to the licence restrictions and confidentiality obligations.

---

## 7. Source-Code Handover

Where Source Code is included in the signed scope and all applicable payment conditions are satisfied, Aöthothe shall provide repository access or an agreed Source-Code archive together with reasonably available build and deployment documentation.

Source-code handover does not include:

- passwords, private keys, recovery codes, or secrets in ordinary repository files;
- third-party materials that cannot lawfully be transferred;
- unrelated Aöthothe repositories, tools, or internal infrastructure;
- undocumented experimental work not accepted into the Project Deliverables;
- ownership of Aöthothe Background IP.

The Client shall keep proprietary Source Code private and grant access only to personnel and contractors who require it and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

---

## 8. Third-Party and Open-Source Components

Third-Party Materials remain subject to their applicable licences and provider terms.

Nothing in this IP Agreement:

- converts open-source software into proprietary Aöthothe property;
- grants rights beyond those permitted by a third-party licence;
- guarantees continued availability or unchanged pricing of a third-party service;
- authorizes removal of required third-party notices.

Aöthothe may replace a third-party component where reasonably necessary for security, compatibility, availability, or licensing compliance.

---

## 9. Attribution and Portfolio Rights

The following attribution should be retained in internal documentation, Source Code notices, system handover records, and formal technical reports:

> **Technology architecture and development by Chrispine Mndala under Aöthothe Technologies.**

Public-facing attribution may be included, modified, or removed by written agreement. Removal from the public interface does not alter the authorship record.

Subject to confidentiality and data-protection obligations, Aöthothe may identify MEC as a client and describe non-confidential aspects of the engagement in its portfolio. Detailed screenshots, analytics, private architecture, personal data, confidential pricing, or credentials require prior written approval.

---

## 10. Assignment Option

No ownership assignment is effective unless the Parties sign a separate written assignment identifying:

- the exact deliverables assigned;
- the economic rights transferred;
- excluded Background IP and Third-Party Materials;
- the assignment price;
- the effective date;
- any retained licence or reuse rights;
- the effect on authorship and attribution.

Unless such an assignment is signed, the operational licence in clause 6 applies.

---

## 11. Protection and Enforcement

Each Party shall promptly notify the other of suspected infringement, unauthorized publication, or misuse of material rights relevant to the platform.

Neither Party may commence enforcement in the other Party’s name without written authority. The Parties may agree on control, cost, settlement, and recovery allocation for any joint enforcement action.

---

## 12. Survival

Clauses concerning authorship, ownership, licence restrictions, confidentiality, third-party rights, enforcement, liability, and dispute resolution survive completion or termination.

---
