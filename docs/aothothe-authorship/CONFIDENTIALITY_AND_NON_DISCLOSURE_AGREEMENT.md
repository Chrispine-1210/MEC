# Mutual Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement

## Mtendere Education Consult Digital Platform

This Mutual Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (the **“NDA”**) is made on the ____ day of __________________ 20____ between:

1. **Aöthothe Technologies**, represented by **Chrispine Mndala** (**“Aöthothe”**); and
2. **Mtendere Education Consult** (**“MEC”** or the **“Client”**).

Each Party may disclose or receive Confidential Information and is therefore a **“Disclosing Party”** or **“Receiving Party”** as applicable.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Parties may exchange Confidential Information for the design, development, testing, deployment, operation, support, maintenance, financing, review, and handover of the MEC digital platform (the **“Authorized Purpose”**).

---

## 2. Confidential Information

**“Confidential Information”** means non-public information disclosed in any form that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person should understand to be confidential in the circumstances, including:

- source code, repositories, scripts, architecture, schemas, technical designs, workflows, and documentation;
- passwords, tokens, private keys, recovery codes, administrator accounts, and security configurations;
- vulnerability reports, incident records, audit findings, logs, and infrastructure details;
- business plans, pricing, quotations, invoices, financial information, strategies, and negotiations;
- student, applicant, customer, staff, partner, sponsor, and user information;
- personal data, uploaded documents, communications, application records, analytics, and operational reports;
- unpublished content, training materials, presentations, contracts, policies, and internal procedures;
- proprietary methods, know-how, reusable components, and product plans of Aöthothe Technologies;
- information belonging to a third party that either Party is obliged to protect.

Confidential Information includes copies, summaries, extracts, analyses, screenshots, exports, and derivative records containing or revealing such information.

---

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

- was lawfully public without breach of this NDA;
- was already lawfully known without confidentiality restriction;
- was independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information;
- was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality restriction;
- was released through the Disclosing Party’s written authorization.

A combination of public elements remains confidential where the specific combination, arrangement, or application is not public.

---

## 4. Receiving Party Obligations

The Receiving Party shall:

1. use Confidential Information only for the Authorized Purpose;
2. protect it using at least reasonable care and no less care than it uses for comparable confidential information of its own;
3. disclose it only to personnel, professional advisers, contractors, and service providers who need it for the Authorized Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations;
4. maintain appropriate access controls and credential security;
5. not publish or store secrets in public repositories, unsecured documents, or unauthorized communication channels;
6. promptly notify the Disclosing Party of confirmed unauthorized access, use, loss, or disclosure;
7. cooperate in reasonable containment, investigation, remediation, and legally required notification;
8. not reverse engineer, extract, copy, or reuse Confidential Information beyond rights granted in a signed agreement.

The Receiving Party is responsible for breaches by persons to whom it discloses Confidential Information, except where responsibility is legally allocated otherwise.

---

## 5. Personal Data

Where Confidential Information includes personal data, each Party shall:

- process it only for a lawful and authorized purpose;
- limit access to persons who require it;
- apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards;
- avoid unnecessary copying or retention;
- follow applicable instructions and legal obligations concerning access, correction, deletion, disclosure, security, and breach response.

This NDA does not replace a data-processing agreement where one is required by the nature or scale of processing.

---

## 6. Compelled Disclosure

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, court order, or lawful regulatory demand, provided that, where legally permitted, it:

- promptly notifies the Disclosing Party;
- limits disclosure to what is legally required;
- reasonably cooperates with requests for protective treatment.

---

## 7. Ownership and No Implied Licence

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party or its applicable rights holder.

Disclosure does not grant ownership, assignment, licence, commercial right, or permission to use intellectual property except as expressly stated in a signed agreement.

The authorship and intellectual-property position of Chrispine Mndala and Aöthothe Technologies is governed by the Authorship and Copyright Notice and the Intellectual Property and Licence Agreement.

---

## 8. Security Credentials

Credentials and secrets must be transferred using a secure method approved by the Parties.

After handover, the Client shall rotate production credentials and remove access that is no longer required. Aöthothe shall not retain active production access beyond the support role authorized in writing.

Neither Party shall request the other to place live passwords, private keys, tokens, or recovery codes in a public repository.

---

## 9. Return, Deletion and Retention

On written request or termination, the Receiving Party shall return or securely delete Confidential Information that is no longer required, subject to:

- legal and regulatory retention obligations;
- ordinary backup cycles;
- professional recordkeeping;
- evidence required for payment, authorship, audit, insurance, security, or dispute purposes;
- continuing support obligations.

Retained information remains protected by this NDA.

---

## 10. Duration

This NDA begins on the Effective Date and continues for the duration of the project and for **five years** after the last disclosure.

Obligations relating to trade secrets, credentials, personal data, proprietary source code, and information that remains confidential by nature continue for as long as the information remains legally protectable or confidential.

---

## 11. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that unauthorized use or disclosure may cause harm not adequately compensated by damages alone. The Disclosing Party may seek urgent injunctive or protective relief in addition to other lawful remedies.

This clause does not create a penalty or exclude any defence available under applicable law.

---

## 12. No Obligation to Proceed

Except for confidentiality and related obligations, this NDA does not require either Party to enter a further transaction, approve a proposal, purchase services, disclose specific information, or continue negotiations.

---

## 13. Governing Law and Disputes

This NDA is governed by the laws of Malawi.

The Parties shall first attempt good-faith negotiation and, where appropriate, mediation. Urgent protective relief may be sought without waiting for negotiation or mediation to conclude.

---

## 14. General

- Amendments must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives.
- A delay in enforcement is not a waiver.
- Invalid provisions shall be severed without invalidating the remainder.
- This NDA may be signed in counterparts and, where legally valid, by electronic signature.
- Neither Party may assign this NDA except with the related business or contract and subject to the successor assuming its obligations.
- This NDA constitutes the entire agreement on confidentiality unless a later signed agreement provides greater protection.
